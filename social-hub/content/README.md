# OkayChat.in social content desk

Research and draft package for owner review. Start with [competitor audit and creative direction](competitor-audit.md), then [30-day calendar](30-day-calendar.md) and [week one post-ready drafts](week-one-drafts.md). Use [review and publishing checklist](review-checklist.md) before anything is posted.

## Editorial guardrails

- Audience: businesses across industries, developers, agencies, SaaS, ecommerce, and customer-support teams.
- Positioning: OkayChat.in is building WhatsApp API and business-messaging tools to help teams organize customer conversations and next steps.
- Palette only: primary green `#16A34A`, dark green `#059669`, charcoal `#0F172A`, pale green `#ECFDF5`, light gray `#F3F4F6`, white `#FFFFFF`. Use Inter. No extra accent colors, including in charts, illustrations, screenshots, emojis, or gradients.
- Product status: workspace, inbox, contacts, leads, campaign drafts, inbound assignment rules, analytics, WhatsApp Cloud API connection, and approved-template send flows are documented in the product. Campaign dispatch, full automation jobs, billing, Embedded Signup, and production readiness/security work remain unfinished. Describe the product as being built and label any UI concept clearly.
- Never claim Meta partnership/certification, official provider status, customer counts, guaranteed results, or availability of unfinished features. Avoid unsupported metrics and customer stories.
- Keep outbound-message examples consent-aware and use approved templates where applicable. Do not imply that a business can message anyone at will.

## Daily drafting rhythm

The 30-day calendar is the daily queue. On each day, prepare one fresh draft from its topic and angle, adapt its format for the selected channels, and leave it marked **Draft — owner review required**. Do not publish automatically. If the day’s topic depends on product status, verify against the repository/product owner before writing.

This package establishes the queue and repeatable review workflow. A scheduled daily reminder/drafting automation needs an owner-selected time and timezone; it has not been configured.
