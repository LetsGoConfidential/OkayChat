**Audience / goal:** all teams; establish a recurring practical habit.

**Visual copy and layout (single graphic or 4-slide carousel):** “A 10-minute conversation reset” with steps: “Find open threads.” “Confirm an owner.” “Write the next step.” “Flag anything waiting on a customer or teammate.” Final: “Small, regular reviews make work visible.”

Design: four-step vertical checklist; pale-green canvas, charcoal headings, green numerals; strong spacing and high contrast.

**Caption:** Try a short weekly reset: review open conversations, confirm who owns each one, write the next step, and flag anything waiting on a reply. Adjust the cadence to your team and customer volume. This is a team habit, not a substitute for a workflow that fits your service needs.
